# Play store listing — the text to paste in

Every field the Play Console asks for, written out, in both languages. It lives
here rather than only in the console so that the next release can diff it, and
so the Spanish is written rather than translated in a hurry at submission time.

**The app name is never translated.** *PictoKeyboard* is the name in both
listings. "PictoTeclado" is not the name of anything.

| Account deletion URL | **leave empty** — the app has no accounts ([`play-data-safety.md`](./play-data-safety.md)) |

**Category, and why not Medical.** *Medical* invites a review that asks for
clinical evidence and regulatory standing this app does not claim to have. It is
a communication tool a caregiver configures, not a diagnostic or therapeutic
device, and describing it as one would be both a harder review and a claim that
is not true. *Education* is where AAC apps sit.

### Short description (80 characters max)

```
A picture keyboard that types and speaks. Built by you, for one person.
```

`70 characters.`

### Full description (4000 characters max)

```
PictoKeyboard turns your phone's keyboard into a grid of pictures. Tap a picture
and it types the word into whatever app you are in — Messages, WhatsApp, email,
notes — and says it out loud.

It is built for people who find pictures easier than letters, and for the
parents, teachers and speech therapists who set it up for them.

WORKS ANYWHERE YOU ALREADY TYPE

PictoKeyboard is a real keyboard, not a separate app you have to copy and paste
out of. Wherever a text field appears, it can be your keyboard, and the words go
straight in.

BUILT AROUND ONE PERSON

Nothing here is a fixed vocabulary handed down to you. Start with the pictogram
set the app comes with, then make it theirs:

• Add pictures from ARASAAC's library of thousands of symbols
• Use your own photographs — their kitchen, their bus stop, their grandmother
• Write what each picture says, and what it speaks, in Spanish or English
• Group pictures into categories, each with its own colour and border
• Set how many rows and columns fit on screen
• Keep several boards for different places or different days

SPEAKS AS IT TYPES

Every picture is spoken as it is tapped, in its own language. A board can mix
Spanish and English, and each word is read in the voice that belongs to it. You
can set the speaking rate and pitch.

FOR PEOPLE WHO CANNOT SEE THE SCREEN

Blind mode turns the keyboard into a gesture surface. Swipe up and down to move
between categories, left and right between pictures, and each one is spoken as
you land on it. Tap to repeat it, double-tap to write it. Two fingers,
double-tap, turns the mode on and off from anywhere.

There is also a high contrast mode — pure black and white, thicker borders and
heavier text — for low vision, and it applies to the keyboard as well as to the
setup screens.

NO ACCOUNT, AND NOTHING LEAVES THE PHONE

There is no sign-up, no login and no server. Boards are stored on the device and
nowhere else. The app collects nothing, sends nothing and has nothing to
collect it to.

To move a board to another phone, or to give one to another family, export it as
a file and send it however you already talk to them — WhatsApp, email, Drive,
Nearby Share. Photographs travel with it. Importing never overwrites what is
already there.

Because nothing is stored online, an exported file is the only backup there is.
Keep one somewhere safe.

SET UP ONCE, THEN LEFT ALONE

Configuration can be put behind a PIN, so the board stays as you built it while
the keyboard itself stays available.

ABOUT THE PICTOGRAMS

The symbols are by Sergio Palao for ARASAAC (http://www.arasaac.org), which
belongs to the Government of Aragón, and are distributed under Creative Commons
BY-NC-SA. PictoKeyboard is free and will stay free — the licence is
non-commercial, and so is this app.
```

```
PictoKeyboard convierte el teclado del móvil en una cuadrícula de imágenes.
Toca una imagen y escribe la palabra en la aplicación en la que estés —
Mensajes, WhatsApp, correo, notas — y la dice en voz alta.

FUNCIONA DONDE YA ESCRIBES

PictoKeyboard es un teclado de verdad, no una aplicación aparte de la que haya
que copiar y pegar. Donde aparezca un campo de texto, puede ser tu teclado, y
las palabras entran directamente.

HECHO PARA UNA PERSONA CONCRETA

Aquí no hay un vocabulario cerrado que te venga dado. Empieza por el conjunto de
pictogramas que trae la aplicación y hazlo suyo:

• Añade imágenes de la biblioteca de ARASAAC, con miles de símbolos
• Usa tus propias fotografías: su cocina, su parada de autobús, su abuela
• Escribe qué dice cada imagen, y qué pronuncia, en español o en inglés
• Agrupa las imágenes en categorías, cada una con su color y su borde
• Ajusta cuántas filas y columnas caben en la pantalla
• Ten varios tableros para sitios distintos o días distintos

HABLA MIENTRAS ESCRIBE

Cada imagen se pronuncia al tocarla, en su propio idioma. Un tablero puede
mezclar español e inglés, y cada palabra se lee con la voz que le corresponde.
Puedes ajustar la velocidad y el tono.

PARA QUIEN NO PUEDE VER LA PANTALLA

El modo ciego convierte el teclado en una superficie de gestos. Desliza arriba y
abajo para cambiar de categoría, a izquierda y derecha para cambiar de imagen, y

cualquier sitio.

También hay un modo de alto contraste — blanco y negro puros, bordes más
marcados y texto más grueso — para baja visión, y se aplica tanto al teclado
como a las pantallas de configuración.

SIN CUENTA, Y NADA SALE DEL MÓVIL

No hay registro, ni inicio de sesión, ni servidor. Los tableros se guardan en el
dispositivo y en ningún otro sitio. La aplicación no recoge nada, no envía nada

Para llevar un tablero a otro móvil, o dárselo a otra familia, expórtalo como
archivo y mándalo por donde ya habléis: WhatsApp, correo, Drive, Nearby Share.
Las fotografías van con él. Importar nunca sobrescribe lo que ya tienes.

Como no se guarda nada en internet, el archivo exportado es la única copia de
seguridad que hay. Guarda una en un sitio seguro.

SE CONFIGURA UNA VEZ Y SE DEJA EN PAZ

La configuración puede quedar detrás de un PIN, para que el tablero siga como lo
montaste mientras el teclado sigue disponible.

SOBRE LOS PICTOGRAMAS
```

## Content rating

The IARC questionnaire is answered in the console and cannot be pre-filled here.
Every answer is **no**: no violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no
controlled substances, no gambling, no user-to-user communication *within the
app*, no sharing of location, and no personal information collected.

**The one to read twice** is "do users interact or exchange content?" The answer
is no. A caregiver exporting a board file and sending it through the system share
sheet is the platform's own sharing, not a channel this app runs — there is no
server, no directory and no way for one user to reach another through it.

Expected outcome: rated for everyone / PEGI 3.

## Target audience

**The audience to declare is adults**, and that is not a dodge. The person
tapping the pictograms may well be a child, but the app is installed,
configured and maintained by an adult, and nothing in it is directed at a child
as a purchaser or as an account holder. Declaring a child audience pulls the
listing into Families policy — which requires, among other things, that the app
not transmit any data, which is already true, and a designed-for-families review
that this app does not need.

Nothing in the app ever asks for the name, age or diagnosis of the person using
the keyboard, which is the fact that matters most for both answers.

## App access

Leave "all functionality is available without special access" — there is no
login. If a PIN has been set on the review device, clear it before uploading a
build for review; a reviewer who meets a PIN they were not given will report the
app as broken.
