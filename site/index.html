PictoKeyboard

Give a voice to anyone who can't type. Tap an ARASAAC pictogram and it types its word into any app and speaks it aloud.

PictoKeyboard is a native Android keyboard. It is set up by a parent, carer or therapist, and used by someone with speech or typing difficulties directly inside WhatsApp, notes, a search bar, or any other text field.

This site exists for the two documents an app has to publish somewhere stable, and for the one thing you should be able to do without installing anything.
