Delete my PictoKeyboard account

This deletes your account from our server, permanently and immediately. There is no grace period and no waiting list.

Your boards are not affected. Boards, categories, pictograms and photographs live in the app's own storage on your phone. They were never on our server, and deleting your account does not touch them. The keyboard keeps working exactly as it does now.

If the app is installed, the same thing is under Settings → Account → Delete my account. This page exists so you can delete your account without needing the app at all.

Want a copy of everything first? The app can export all your boards and photographs as a single .pkb file. It is easier to do that while the account still exists.

Step 1 — prove it is your account

We ask you to sign in for one reason: so that nobody but you can delete your account. We never delete an account named in a form — only the one that has signed in.
